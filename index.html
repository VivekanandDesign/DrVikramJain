Services Offered

Entire spectrum of autoimmune rheumatic diseases including adult and pediatric, along with soft tissue rheumatism, degenerative joint disorders and allergies are routinely managed. These include:

Systemic connective tissue diseases

  • Systemic Lupus Erythematosus
  • Primary Sjogren syndrome
  • Primary antiphospholipid syndrome
  • Scleroderma
  • Mixed connective tissue diseases
  • UCTD and overlap syndromes
  • Systemic Vasculitis
  • Inflammatory muscle disorders
  • Rare disorders: Behcets, Sarcoidosis, Amyloid etc

Inflammatory Arthritis

    Rheumatoid Arthritis
  • Spondyloarthropathies
  • Psoriatic arthritis
  • Crystal arthritis – gout , CPPD

Pediatric Immunology

  • Juvenile idiopathic arthritis
  • Primary immunodeficiency disorders

Soft tissue rheumatism

    Fibromyalgia
  • Complex regional pain syndrome
  • Myofascial syndromes

Degenerative joint disorders

  • Osteoarthritis

Allergic disorders

    Chronic urticaria/angioedema
  • Allergic rhinitis/conjunctivitis

Miscellaneous

  • Osteoporosis, metabolic bone diseases
  • Single organ immune disorders – eg. Uveitis, scleritis, interstitial lung disease

Diagnostic Procedures

We offer advanced diagnostic procedures, including ultrasound-guided techniques, to ensure precise and effective care for every patient.

Biopsy

Kidney, skin, and minor salivary gland biopsies for accurate tissue diagnosis.

Joint Aspiration & Crystal Exam

Fluid removal and analysis to diagnose arthritis and crystal-related conditions.

Autologous Serum Skin Test

Specialized allergy test for chronic urticaria and related disorders.

Pathergy Test

Diagnostic test for Behçet's disease and other immune conditions.

Joint & Soft Tissue Ultrasound

Real-time imaging for precise diagnosis and guided procedures.

Therapeutic

The department offers specialized immunomodulatory therapy including high dose steroids, biological therapy and intravenous immunoglobulin for the management of autoimmune and immunodeficiency disorders. Joint and soft tissue injections for inflammatory arthritis and soft tissue rheumatism are also routinely performed.

Drug Therapy

  • Cyclophosphamide infusion therapy
  • Methylprednisolone infusion therapy
  • Zolendronic acid infusion therapy
  • Biological therapy (Infliximab, Etanercept, Rituximab, Tocilizumab)
  • Intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIg) infusion

Intra-articular and soft tissue injections
